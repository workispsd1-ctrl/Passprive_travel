Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This service is operated by Passprive Platforms Private Ltd ("PassPrivé", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PassPrivé Travel to plan, customize, or book your trip, we collect information necessary to deliver travel services:

  • Contact Information: Your name, WhatsApp phone number, and email address to share custom itinerary proposals, hotel vouchers, and booking updates.
  • Trip Preferences: Travel dates, traveler type (couples, families, or groups), group size, accommodation preferences, and flight requirements.
  • On-Ground Service Details: Flight arrival and departure details strictly for scheduling your private chauffeur airport pick-ups and excursions.
  • PassPrivé App Usage Data: Reservation timestamps and participating partner dining bill redemption tokens for cashback processing.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Creating, refining, and sharing your personalized Mauritius day-by-day itinerary proposal.
  • Confirming hotel accommodations, private transport logistics, and activity permits on your behalf.
  • Activating complimentary VIP access to the PassPrivé App for on-island dining reservations and cashback privileges.
  • Providing responsive on-ground assistance and trip coordination via WhatsApp during your stay.

3. Zero Spam & Data Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks. We only disclose essential booking data to vetted on-ground partners (licensed Mauritius transport chauffeurs, verified hotels, and certified excursion providers) solely to execute your confirmed travel itinerary.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and operational safeguards to protect your personal details. Trip planning correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill your trip, maintain legal compliance, and resolve post-travel queries.

5. Your Rights & Erasure Requests

You have the right to inspect, update, or request the immediate deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise your rights, contact our privacy compliance team at concierge@passprive.travel or write to Passprive Platforms Private Ltd. Deletion requests are processed within 48 hours.
